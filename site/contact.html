Contact and feedback

Bug reports, feature ideas and questions all help shape FileDrop.

Email

Write to our support address (coming soon). We usually reply within two business days, in English or French.

Reporting a bug

Please include:

  • Your FileDrop version (bottom of the admin window's sidebar).
  • Your Windows version, and the phone or browser that had the problem.
  • What you did, what you expected and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot, if it helps. If you attach the log file from %ProgramData%\FileDrop\logs\filedrop.log, check it first: it contains usernames, device addresses and times, but never file contents or passwords.

Feature ideas

Tell us what you'd use FileDrop for and what's missing. We read everything, especially during the beta.

Privacy requests

To access, correct or delete information you've sent us, email us with the subject "Privacy request". See the privacy policy for your rights.
