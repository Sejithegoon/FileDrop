Summary

FileDrop runs on your own Windows computer. Your files, user accounts and settings are stored on that computer, not with us. The app does not send us your files, file names or usage data. We collect only the limited information needed to run this website, answer you and, once Pro launches, sell and support licences.

We follow Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). Where they apply, we also respect Quebec's Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector (Law 25), the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and US state privacy laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

Information in the FileDrop app

  • Files: stored in the folder the administrator chooses on their PC. Other people reach them over the local network. Files are never sent to us.
  • Accounts: usernames, display names and passwords (stored only as Argon2id hashes) are kept in a database on the PC, in %ProgramData%\FileDrop.
  • Activity and logs: sign-ins, admin actions and device network addresses are recorded on the PC so the administrator can manage it. They stay on the PC.
  • Update check: if turned on, FileDrop downloads a small version file from our website about once a day. Like any web request, this shows our web host your IP address and the app version. Nothing else is sent.
  • No telemetry: the app does not send analytics, crash reports, file names or file contents.

The person who runs FileDrop on their PC decides how it's used and is responsible for the information of the people they add. We have no access to it.

Information on this website

  • Server logs: our web host records basic request information (IP address, browser, pages requested and time) to deliver and protect the site. Logs are kept for no more than [30] days.
  • Cookies: we don't use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. The site stores your light or dark theme choice in your own browser, which is never sent to us.
  • Pro waitlist: if you sign up, we keep your email address and the date you gave consent, and use them only to tell you when Pro launches. Every email includes our contact details and an unsubscribe link, as Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL) requires. We delete your address when you unsubscribe or [12] months after launch, whichever comes first.
  • Messages: if you email us, we keep your message and address to reply and to improve FileDrop, for up to [24] months.

Purchases and licences (when Pro launches)

  • Payments will be handled by [payment provider], acting as merchant of record. They process your name, email, billing address and payment details under their own privacy policy. We never see full card numbers.
  • Our licence service stores a licence ID, order ID, edition, update period, refund status and up to three random installation IDs, so licences can be activated, recovered and refunded. It never receives file names or contents.
  • We keep purchase records for as long as tax law requires (in Canada, generally six years).

Why we use information (legal bases)

We use information only for the purposes above. For visitors in the EU and UK, our legal bases are: consent (waitlist emails), contract (selling and supporting licences), legal obligation (tax records) and legitimate interests (keeping the website secure and answering messages). We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't use it for targeted advertising.

Where information is stored

This website is hosted on servers in Canada by [hosting provider]. Some service providers we may use (such as the payment provider and email service) may process information in other countries, including the United States, where it may be accessible to local authorities under local law. When information leaves Canada or the European Economic Area, we rely on contracts with appropriate safeguards, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

Security

We collect as little as possible, use encrypted connections (HTTPS), limit access to the few people who need it and use reputable providers. If a breach creates a real risk of significant harm, we'll notify affected people and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada as the law requires.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to tell you what information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop using it for emails. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to data portability, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. We answer within 30 days.

If you're not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec, or your local data protection authority in the EU or UK.

Children

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. Parents and guardians who run FileDrop at home decide who uses it on their own network.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For important changes, we'll also post a notice on this website.
