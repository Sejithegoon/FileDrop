Refund policy

Last updated October 16, 2026.

Draft for legal review. This page has been prepared in good faith but has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer. Items in [square brackets] must be completed before launch.

FileDrop Pro isn't on sale yet. This is the refund policy we plan to use when it launches.

30-day refund guarantee

If FileDrop Pro isn't right for you, ask for a full refund within 30 days of purchase. You don't need to give a reason.

How to ask

Reply to your purchase receipt or use the contact page, and include your order number. Refunds go back to your original payment method through our payment provider, usually within 5 to 10 business days.

What happens after a refund

Your Pro licence is cancelled, and Pro features turn read-only the next time FileDrop checks your licence. Your files, accounts and every FileDrop Free feature keep working.

Your legal rights

This policy is in addition to any rights you have under the consumer laws where you live.
