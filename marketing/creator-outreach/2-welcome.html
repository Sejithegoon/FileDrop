WHAT IT IS

WiFiDrop turns a Windows laptop or PC with lots of free space into a private backup for phones. Open a web page on any phone on the same Wi-Fi and send photos, videos and files straight to the PC, full quality. Nothing goes to the cloud, there's no subscription, and the files stay on your own drive.

GET STARTED (5 MINUTES)

1 Download WiFiDrop for Windows 10 or 11 from wifidrop.ca/download.
2 Run the installer. The beta isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may say "Windows protected your PC": click More info → Run anyway.
3 In the WiFiDrop window, pick where files should be saved (a folder on your big drive), then go to Licence and paste the licence above.
4 Add an account under Users, then scan the QR code on the Dashboard with your phone and start sending photos.
